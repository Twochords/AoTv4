# AoTv4 Patch Notes — 9 August 2026

## Combat

- Nothing runs away any more — every creature fights to the death.
- Nothing enrages any more.
- Combat abilities no longer generate bonus threat. Bash is the exception and now makes **twice** as much.
- Carrying a shield gives **+50% threat** on every swing and every spell.
- Bash hits harder with a shield, and a better shield hits harder still.
- If a guard or another monster lands the killing blow, you get no experience, no loot and no quest credit.

## Items

- Two-handed weapons hit for about **15% less** damage.
- Bows, arrows and thrown weapons had their range corrected.
- Items you cannot equip no longer show pointless stats. Augments and tributes are unaffected.
- Healing potions have been removed.

## Classes and Skills

- **Every class can now use Alchemy, Tinkering and Make Poison** — including training them at a trainer.
- New characters now start with their skills already at cap, and every tradeskill at 20.
- Bard songs no longer disappear when you zone.

## Towns

- New faction **"Titan's Resistance"** — every race is now friendly in every town.
- No more being unable to sell in your own home town.

## Delves

- The Lost Dungeons half of the delve ladder is enterable again.
- Traps no longer trigger in delves.
- Cursed barrels and chests no longer appear in delves.
- Raid-sized dungeons have been removed from the delve rotation.
- The boss actually has to be killed — trash kills no longer finish the run.
- Your journal names the dungeon you are actually in.
- Dragons of Norrath delves no longer need far more kills than Lost Dungeons ones.

## Spells

- Fear spells are no longer offered as level-up rewards, and monsters no longer cast fear.
- Camouflage, Invoke Lightning, Harmony and similar spells now work indoors. Mounts are unchanged.
- Monsters no longer cast the debuff that made you miss almost every swing.
- **Aura of Shadows** and **Aura of Fury** were only working with a 1H Blunt weapon. Fixed.
- Class auras reworked — most now scale instead of giving a flat number that stopped mattering.

## World

- Experience rates adjusted per zone.
- New **Zone XP** tab in the Allaclone window shows where to hunt, by region and level.
- Legendary and Fabled monsters have been turned off for now.
- Quest experience rewards rescaled.
