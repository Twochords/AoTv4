## 2. What was built — Titanwrought mold crafting (v152-v157)
Full design and reasoning: **`TITANWROUGHT_CRAFTING_DESIGN.md`** (§13 there is the build record).

| migration | what | ids |
|---|---|---|
| v152 | 86 gear items completing the slot map | 148000-148085 |
| v153 | 210 molds, one per craftable output | Crude 148200-148269 · Simple ...339 · Rough ...409 |
| v154 | 48 materials (36 bases, 9 bindings, 3 tempers) | 148500-148547 |
| v155 | 255 recipes (45 sub + 210 combines) | 480000-480254 |
| v156 | hub vendor stock on Audri_Deepfacet (202069) | slots 144-149 |
| v157 | mold drops off named mobs; **disables the gear drops** | global_loot 101-102 |

**The shape of the system:** a mold (slot + type + tier) plus a base, a binding and a bought temper
makes one piece of Titanwrought. Combines **never fail** (`nofail`); tradeskill **skill decides the
tier** (native/Hallowed/Mythic) through the existing `AoTv4RollCraftTier`. The **mold is consumed**
and **does not survive death**.

- Generated by **`custom/tools/gen_titanwrought_crafting.py`** — re-runnable, rewrites all four SQL
  files. Do not hand-edit the SQL.
- Lua: `aotv4_dungeon.M.roll_mold` (delve chest, rungs 1-20, **10 percent** — `M.MOLD_CHANCE`),
  `aotv4_raid.M.grant_molds` (**2** Rough molds per raider). `death_loss.lua` deliberately does
  **not** exempt molds.
- Mold sources: Crude/Simple from delve rungs **and** open-world **named** mobs (`global_loot`
  `rare = 1`); **Rough only from raid bosses**, placed in Lua with no loot row at all.

⚠️⚠️ **THE GEAR NO LONGER DROPS.** `global_loot` 1-4 are disabled, so until somebody crafts, no
Titanwrought enters the world. That is the design, and it is the most visible change to anyone who
was farming it. One `UPDATE` reverses it if you want both for a while.

⚠️⚠️⚠️ **THE TITANWROUGHT GEAR SHIPPED WITH NO HALLOWED OR MYTHIC ROWS, AND THAT KILLED THE WHOLE
SKILL-PICKS-THE-TIER MECHANIC (found and fixed 2026-08-30).** v152 created the 86 base items but
nothing re-ran `custom/sql/aotv4_gear_tiers.sql`, which is what generates the tier copies — so
448000-448085 and 748000-748085 did not exist. `AoTv4RollCraftTier` swaps a combine's output for its
Hallowed/Mythic tier **only when one exists** (§32: the "does a Mythic exist" lookup *is* the wearable
test), so with no rows the swap silently no-op'd and **every Titanwrought craft produced base tier at
any tradeskill skill.** The headline mechanic of §64 was inert from the day it shipped.
- 📌 It was fixed as a **side effect**: the item-scaling re-run forced §35's mandatory
  `aotv4_gear_tiers.sql` → `aotv4_craft_sockets.sql` → `./shared_memory` follow-up, and that generated
  the missing 86 pairs. Tier count went 26,424 → **26,510**, which is exactly +86.
- ✅ Verified: all 86 have Hallowed at AC ×1.5 and Mythic at AC ×2 / HP ×2.5, and none is weaker than
  its base.
- ⚠️⚠️ **ANY FUTURE BATCH OF CRAFTABLE GEAR NEEDS `aotv4_gear_tiers.sql` RE-RUN, THEN
  `aotv4_craft_sockets.sql`, THEN `./shared_memory`.** Creating the items is only half of it, and the
  missing half is invisible: the item exists, the recipe works, the combine succeeds, and the tier
  roll just never fires. Nothing errors.
- 📌 The generator does pick these up on its own — its scope includes recipe outputs, not just
  `lootdrop_entries`/`merchantlist` (Titanwrought is in **none** of the loot or merchant tables). So it
  is stable from here; it simply had never been run since the gear was created.

## 5. ⚠️ WHAT IS BUILT BUT HAS NEVER BEEN PLAYED
This is the §30 distinction and it matters — all of the below is verified against the **data**, not
in game. **Nobody has crafted anything, and nobody has fought a raid boss.**

1. **The first craft.** Sharp test: a **Crude Cloth Cap**. Buy a Crude Temper (100p) and Silk Swatch
   from **Audri_Deepfacet** in Freeport Theater, craft a Crude Silk Bolt (Tailoring) and a Crude
   Armour Fitting (Pottery), get a mold from a delve rung 1-10, combine at a loom.
   Confirm: the combine **cannot fail**; the **mold is consumed**; higher skill gives a **better
   tier**, not a better success rate; and — the one §32's trap breaks — the recipe is actually
   **findable in the recipe search window**.
2. **A raid boss.** The hp numbers are arithmetic from an estimate, not a measured kill.
   ⚠️⚠️ **Phinigel could not be entered AT ALL until 2026-08-30** — his encounter row carried
   `zoneid = 66` (`gukbottom`, Lower Guk) against a `zone` of `"kedge"` (64), so the instance was
   built for Kedge and the party sent to Guk; world refused them and safe-returned them to where they
   stood. It reads as "the raid did not start", with no error anywhere. The two fields are now
   reconciled by `aotv4_raid.M.zone_id_of`, which resolves from the short name and reports a
   mismatch. **Mayong (59) and Velketor (112) were always correct**, so try one of those first if a
   raid misbehaves again — it isolates encounter data from the shared entry path.
3. **Mold drop rates.** Retuned 2026-08-30 (owner's call): the delve chest went from **guaranteed to
   10 percent** and raid molds from **3 to 2**; named mobs stay at `probability 25`. All three were
   originally sized while molds were assumed to persist through death, and they no longer do — so
   these are now deliberately *thinner* than that sizing. Judge in play; the levers are
   `aotv4_dungeon.M.MOLD_CHANCE` and `aotv4_raid.M.MOLD_DROPS`, one number each.
4. ⚠️ **The Rough mold is the sharpest corner**: raid-only, 24-hour lockout, consumed per combine,
   and destroyed on death. The intended loop is *raid, then craft before you die*. If that is too
   punishing the fix is one line in `death_loss.M.is_kept`.
