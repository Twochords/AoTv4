# AoTv4 Patch Notes — 7 August 2026

## Tradeskills

**There is now a three-piece tradeskill outfit.** You earn each piece from a tradeskill achievement:

| Piece | Earned at | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Head | skill 100 | +5% |
| Face mask | skill 200 | +10% |
| Gloves | skill 300 | +15% |

- **Only your best piece counts.** Wearing all three is the same as wearing just the gloves.
- **The bonus now shows on your skill sheet.** It was always working, you just could not see it.
- **Wear the mask on your face.** It used to sit in your bags. It has to be worn to do anything.
- **At 300 skill with the gloves on, everything you craft comes out Mythic.**
- **388 more recipes now show up in the recipe search**, mostly Pottery and Planes of Power armour.

## Delves

- **The Lost Dungeons delves are enterable again.** Every even-numbered delve was refusing to let you
  in. All 70 levels now work.
- **Your quest journal names the right dungeon.** It was showing the wrong name most of the time.
  Your kills always counted — only the name was wrong.
- **Veksar is no longer a delve map.**

## New: the Zone XP tab

Open the **Allaclone** window and click **Zone XP** to see where to hunt. It lists every zone by
region with the levels you will find there.

- Browse the whole list, or type to search for a zone.
- Cities are marked "City" instead of a level range.
- Delves are not listed — their creatures match your level, so there is no fixed range to show.

## Fixes

- **Tradeskill achievements were almost never giving their rewards** — the mastery AAs, the head
  piece and the mask. The achievement completed and gave you nothing.
  - **If this happened to you, your rewards are waiting.** Type **`#ach claim`** to collect them, or
    just finish any achievement and they will arrive.
- **The masks would not work at all.** Clicking one said "Item is out of charges". They work now, and
  the illusion lasts 30 minutes instead of 3.
- **Tradeskill items all had the same cloth hat icon.** Each piece has its own icon now.
- **Autoskills were firing the same attack several times at once.** They fire once.
- **Bulwark Within** now gives 300 hit points, 300 mana and 300 endurance. It used to give less mana
  and endurance than it did hit points.
  - Warriors, Monks, Rogues and Berserkers do not get the mana — their mana does not take bonuses.
    They still get the hit points and endurance.
